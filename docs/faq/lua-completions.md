# Lua completions

Kitsune has completions for lua LSPs. You can grab them [here](https://codeberg.org/LunarWatcher/kitsune/src/branch/master/meta). Depending on how you downloaded kitsune, your download package may or may not include them.

They are currently luals-compatible, but since luals is based on AI slop, this may change as soon as a non-AI-slop-based lua LSP comes around.
